# SangoPass brand

SangoPass connects visitor access, residents and property operations for South African student residences, apartments and residential communities.

## Visual identity

- Forest `#143E35`: navigation, primary actions, headings and trust.
- Ivory `#FAFAF6`: public pages and warm backgrounds.
- Lime `#D5ED9F`: selected navigation and highlights on forest. Use dark text on lime.
- Sage `#71884C`: supporting accents. Body copy uses darker text for readability.
- Geist typography, generous spacing, clear labels, rounded cards, consistent status badges.
- The logo combines an open doorway and verification tick. Editable assets: `public/brand/sangopass-mark.svg`, `public/brand/sangopass-logo.svg`, and `components/ui/Brand.tsx`.

## South African context

Use South African English, rand amounts, local phone examples and `Africa/Johannesburg` for visit dates and times. Imagery should represent the rainbow nation naturally: people from different backgrounds sharing ordinary community life, locally recognisable buildings and indigenous planting. These generated scenes are illustrative, not customer photos or endorsements.

## Imagery

All four images were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. PNG originals and compressed WebP website assets are saved in `public/brand/`. No external hotlinks or API keys are needed to display them.
